# QuaNTecH-ViD brand system

## Product boundary

QuaNTecH-ViD is an open-source video production studio for podcast creators. SecuredMe stewards the project while integrations with education suites, media platforms, and AI providers remain capability-based and optional.

## Identity

- **Name:** QuaNTecH-ViD
- **Promise:** Voice, made visual.
- **Audience:** podcast creators and knowledge publishers.
- **Tone:** professional studio, calm technical control, visible creative authority.
- **Base:** `#07111F` ink.
- **Signal:** `#2DD4FF` cyan.
- **Motion:** `#8A5CFF` violet.
- **Attention:** `#FFB454` amber.
- **Text:** `#EDF5FF` and `#A9BDD3`.

## Creative requirement

Every episode should feel authored rather than templated. Reusable identity lives in typography, rhythm, colour, transitions, avatar, voice, and evidence treatment. Scene composition remains adaptable to the subject.

## Voice and avatar

- A creator can record, import, or connect an approved voice profile.
- A creator can create or import an avatar through a supported provider.
- Consent, permitted uses, provenance, deletion, and revocation remain visible.
- Generated media receives an appropriate disclosure in its manifest.
- Identity assets stay scoped to the creator and selected project.

## Provider integration contract

The side panel may adapt its controls to Google, OpenAI, or another provider while preserving QuaNTecH-ViD’s project schema, approvals, voice identity, avatar identity, and QA report. Provider availability can change; the UI displays capability and readiness before each action.

## UI rules

1. Show source, active provider, cost class, and destination.
2. Reserve cyan and violet for actions and live signals.
3. Reserve amber for attention, approval, capacity, or ambiguity.
4. Keep forms spacious and labelled in plain language.
5. Make every provider error actionable and typed.
6. Require explicit approval before rendering, publishing, voice cloning, or avatar creation.
